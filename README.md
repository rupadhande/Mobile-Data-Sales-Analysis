# 📱 Mobile Sales Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard presents a comprehensive analysis of mobile phone sales across multiple cities and brands, month-over-month. It delivers valuable insights through KPIs, trend lines, pie and bar charts. The dashboard supports filters for month, country/city, and brand with storage size to enable deep-dive analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI** – Data visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Excel/CSV** – Source data

## 📌 Storytelling Insights

1. 📈 **Overall Sales Performance**
   - The dashboard highlights strong mobile sales with **Total Revenue of 15 Million** and **Total Quantity Sold of 19K units**.
   - This indicates healthy sales volume and good market demand across regions.

2. 📅 **Sales Trend by Month**
   - The **Sales Trend by Month** line chart shows that **July** had the **highest sales**, while **April** and **September** experienced noticeable dips.
   - A visible **upward trend in the second half of the year** suggests positive seasonality, likely driven by festive or promotional periods.
   - Months like **January, July, October, and December** are above average (indicated by the dotted trend line).
   - This trend could guide future marketing or promotional campaigns.

3. 🏆 **Brand Performance**
   - Samsung is the **top-selling brand**, contributing **25.08%** to total sales.
   - Google and OnePlus also hold strong shares: **23.97%** and **20.84%** respectively.
   - Apple, despite its reputation, contributed only **12.53%**, indicating a potential area to explore pricing, features, or market strategies.

4. 🌍 **City-wise Sales**
   - The **bar chart** shows that **Bursa** had the **highest sales**, followed by **Chennai** and **Amritsar**.
   - Cities like **Lahore**, **Multan**, and **Islamabad** had the lowest sales – signaling untapped markets or low brand penetration.
   - High-performing cities could be analyzed to replicate success models in weaker regions.

5. 🎯 **Opportunities for Business Action**
   - **Improve Apple’s market share** through targeted promotions or better feature positioning.
   - Investigate **April and September** to understand the dip in sales — are they off-peak months, or is there an operational issue?
   - Explore **low-performing cities** with market research to understand gaps.
   - **Double down on strong months** like July and December with added campaigns or product launches.
